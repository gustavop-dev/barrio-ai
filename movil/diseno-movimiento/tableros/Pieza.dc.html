TEXTO FINAL

AI features should start from a workflow, not a model.

Last month a founder asked me to “add AI” to their onboarding.

Before picking a model, we mapped the five steps a new customer takes and where they get stuck.

Only one step needed AI: turning a messy spreadsheet into a clean import.

That’s the feature we built. It took two weeks, not two months.

Start from the workflow. The model is an implementation detail.

TEXTO FINAL

Turning a sales call into a product spec.

The best spec I wrote last month started as a sales call.

Not the transcript: the three decisions the buyer kept circling.

Each decision became one screen in the first version.

Everything else went to a “later” list the buyer could see.

Specs are easier to agree on when they come from the buyer’s own words.
